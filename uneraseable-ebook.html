Scene 1 · The Highway ● Critique Added

The air inside the coach tasted of old foam cushions, ozone, and red desert dust. Sunlight spilled down through the overhead vents in long, trembling shafts, illuminating the suspended silt that rose whenever the tires struck a seam in the sun-bleached asphalt.

Up front, Doug shifted down into third gear. The transmission groaned with the heavy weight of twenty passengers who had nowhere else left to go. Overhead, the television monitors buzzed with quiet grey static.
