00:00 / 00:00

THE NIGHT OF EMBERS

Apex Arena Radio Special Broadcast

Valerius Sterling reports live alongside Vumbua Harmonics Expert Professor Kante following the catastrophic sector barrier drop.

💡 Tap any statement below to jump the broadcast directly to that line!
🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 0m 00s

And we are BACK! Power is finally restored across Vumbua! Valerius Sterling here, coming to you live from the recovered Ops center of the Apex Arena—and while our technicians were frantically kicking the resonators back to life, reports have been flooding in from every sector in Harmony about strange little power blips. People are already calling it The Night of Embers—well, at least I am. remember listeners, if that catches on: you heard it here first!

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 0m 33s

Now, for our listeners who were eagerly tuned into the Academy’s baseline field survival trial in the Apex Arena: let me tell you... it did NOT go according to the brochure! The Academy’s official intent was to test our new cadets' raw survival instincts in a controlled environment. Simulating a crash landing in the wilderness with zero preparation time, scraps were scattered across the canyon floor, and a tiny, heavily monitored trickle of low-threat beasts was to be let through the perimeter gates, this would give the Captains a good chance to see the first-years in action. Cadets were given fair warning to evacuate if they couldn't handle the heat. About a quarter of them took the hatch! But that still left thirty-five thousand eager candidates who stayed inside to test themselves against the challenge Vumbua had in store.

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 1m 34s

And what a show it was! We saw high-speed chases, zero-sum brawls over raw sulfur caches, improvised glider rigs, trench-digging, deep diving—every insane form of transport and salvage you can imagine! All setting up for what was supposed to be a textbook crash-landing survival exercise... which brings us to the part where the sky fell down and things got a bit more.. realistic than planned. I have with me Vumbua harmonics expert Professor Kante to give us an idea of what the blazes happened last night.

🔬 PROFESSOR KANTE ▶ Jump to 2m 12s

Thank you Valerious. You see the main grid capacitors were ramped up to increase resonance resistance and dampen repulsor output for the beast-entry phase; however, when the umber crystal resistors were cycled back down, the planned replacement resonance failed to materialize. This triggered a systemic harmonic desynchronization across the panda 5 cell array, as demand surged over supply, resulting in a total localized drop of the sector barrier.

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 2m 42s

Uh huh. I'll be honest listeners, I may need you to tell me what that means, because I’ll tell ya—I haven't got the foggiest!

🔬 PROFESSOR KANTE ▶ Jump to 2m 52s

Bottom Line, Valerius? There was a temporary lapse in the amount of resonance required to run the Vumbua shield.

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 3m 01s

...Well that sounds... scary. What does this mean for the regular Harmony citizen? Are we in danger of these repeating?

🔬 PROFESSOR KANTE ▶ Jump to 3m 09s

For now, it will mean very little. Large resonance consumers like Resonance Race Arenas will need to carefully monitor any cycling or consider more continuous draw. But for the average citizen, you will not experience anything different. Citizens need not worry, I have great faith in our faculty Vumbua has accelerated the Ventures. They will find the answers to this! I am particularly excited about what Professor Ink has in mind, but I should not say any more here. Rest assured, Vumbua will move forward.

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 3m 47s

Well you certainly are a comfort and a bit of a tease, Professor! In any case, thank you for translating for the layman here. We look forward to reporting on what happens next!

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 4m 01s

Now... on a much more somber note. It is now painfully clear that when that barrier dropped, the aetheric beast horde outside didn't care about trial rules. Not every candidate made it out of the trap we built for them. Official casualty numbers are still being tallied by Dean Vane’s office, but... we are all holding our breath and praying at least half made it through this ordeal. It is a devastating blow for the Academy, for the parents, and for the future of our adventuring class.

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 4m 39s

But if there is one dark, silver lining to emerge from the embers today... The cadets who walked out of that clearing this morning aren't just students anymore. They are hardened survivors—more ready to face the raw, unmapped Wilderness than anyone who has come before them, and it sounds like the Academy is recognizing this with the acceleration of the Venture. I am sure the survivors will find no issues discovering a Captain to take them on.

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 5m 13s

For them, we hope this fuels their momentum, I get the feeling we will alll be needing it.

🎙️ VALERIUS STERLING ▶ Jump to 5m 21s

Valerius Sterling - signing off.
