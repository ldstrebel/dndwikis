Zephyr, Britt, Loami (chasing his antidote with engine grease!), Saffron, Pip (running up and hitting her forehead straight against the trunk!), and Aggie all drank their vials and pressed their bare palms and foreheads to the taboo petroglyph mandala. Violet lichen erupted into a blinding vortex of teal light, pulling their senses into the ancestral network vision.

Three hundred years into the past, the vision materialized.

They stood on a jet-black volcanic sand beach under a heavy tropical mist. To the left lay a damp, cold rainforest where primitive forest dwellers—the Mizizi, carrying turtle-like skin and living moss robes—shivered beneath colossal leaves. To the right, volcanic magma crags roared with heat as Ash-Blood islanders with smoldering obsidian skin and crowns of orange flame huddled around open lava vents.

From the tree line stepped Mwangi, the regal leader of the Mizizi, gripping a petrified ironbark staff. From the lava vents walked Brakkan, the smoldering Ash-Blood chief clad in beaten bronze scale armor. They met in silence on the neutral black sand shore.

Knowing no words, each chief drew the elemental spark of their tribe into the wet black earth. Brakkan carved a stepped sun-spire fire arc into the wet sand. Mwangi stepped forward, striking his ironbark staff straight through the arc to carve a vertical root-ladder spine. A connection was born.

"The tree remembers the fire!"

Mwangi declared.

"The fire remembers the tree!"

Brakkan shouted back.

Connecting the two peoples unleashed an explosion of joint creation. Mizizi wood-weavers and Ash-Blood thermal smiths built the world's first comfortable, heated ironbark home, chisel-carving the combined two-stroke mandala mark above the main arch.

The vision pulled them into subterranean ocean caverns, where Mwangi brought thermal lamps to Barnaby of the Trench-Kin. Barnaby struck the third stroke—a stepped ocean wave spiral—into the basalt keystone, forging underwater ocean cities.

Above the clouds, Astra of the Fulgur tempest riders joined the alliance, striking the fourth stroke—the thunderbird lightning-zigzag—into the step-pyramid capstone. In sandstone canyons, the Wadi cultivated terraced gardens. Atop suspended sky-ziggurats, Seraphina of the Renali placed a turquoise crystal sphere into the altar, completing the Master Glyph.

Empress Relina Vox ruled over a golden apex era of prosperity. But centuries of ease brought decay. Unmaintained sky plazas and conduits fractured. The five elemental sparks degraded, causing weapons to fail and forcing clans into crude medieval warfare.

To prevent total extinction, scholars formulated The Exchange—sealing the six sparks into isolation to keep the smallest flame alive. The coalition clear-cut the petrified forest, causing centuries of shared memories to fade naturally from the public mind as petrified bark turned to dust.

The vision snapped back to the present. Britt and Aggie sat up gasping on the forest floor of Center Grove as violet lichen faded back to quiet stone. Iggy opened his copper goggles, looking around at the team in profound awe.

"Isolation is never the answer,"

Iggy whispered softly, his hands pressed into the damp soil.

Professor Kante’s voice crackled through the communication stone.

"Professor Inc.! Cadets! We just registered a massive resonance spike across the Panda 5 cell array! What did you do down there?!"

Professor Inc. and Rill watched in awe as fresh green sprouts and tiny trees erupted around Pip's and Loami's feet.

"Bramble! Bramble!"

Pip cheered, skipping around the clearing and touching the ground as miniature trees sprouted everywhere.

"I'm like you! I'm making the plants go bramble!"

Rill looked at the growing network in wonder.

"If we find them, now we know where they are! If we connect them all, then we'll stabilize the resonance decay and the clans can come out—but this time, we just can't stop. I get it now. Momentum is life."

In the clearing center, Mwaza-Kasa's living shell plates displayed the organic world map of Vumbua, glowing with pinprick runes marking the unmapped territories of the lost clans.

Iggy crouched beside the tortoise, staring at the glowing scute lines before pointing a trembling hand at the ocean territory.

"My whole life is a lie!"

Loami crouched beside him, staring at the glowing scutes.

"Look at the shell... it's the whole map. Arc two starts right here."
