Alfie's Origin: The Driftwood Construct & The Burning Coast

Who carved the driftwood construct, and whose memories of a burning coastline are trapped in her cedar grain?

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 1] Woven by the Fates: Crafted from weathered coastal driftwood and drowned sailors' cloaks in the Library of the Three Fates.
  • [Session 1] 458 Years Old: Carries four and a half centuries of memories embedded deep inside her timber.
  • [Session 3 & 7] Flashbacks of Salt & Fire: Touching ancient sea-carved stone triggers terrifying sensory visions—freezing saltwater, silent oarsmen in the dark, and a coastal village burning to the waterline.
  • [Session 7] Wordcraft Power: Wields the Fates' editing magic—able to rewrite reality by swapping a single letter in a word—which she used to sever the Gorgon's tendrils in the Lost Roads.

The Raleigh Aegean Stele: The Stone That Shocks

An ancient Greek stone stolen from a North Carolina museum vault, carrying the violent memories of an erased sea battle.

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 3] The Raleigh Museum Heist: Snatched from the high-security vault of a museum in Raleigh, North Carolina; carved with ancient Aegean Greek lettering.
  • [Session 3] Touch & Terror: Anyone who touches the stone bare-handed is hit with an electric jolt and blinding visions of blood, rowing benches, and bronze-tipped warships.
  • [Session 4] The Sleepless Nights: Keeping the stone near camp causes severe insomnia and dread; the party has to burn oil lanterns all night just to get a few hours of sleep.
  • [Session 4] An Anchor in the World: Naomi confirmed the stone is a physical anchor—as long as the stone survives, the historical battle carved into it cannot be completely erased by the Redactors.

Dr. Thorne's Notebook: The 1948 Big Pox Alteration

An old clinical trial binder recovered behind a university stage, where a single word was changed in ink to rewrite history.

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 5] Found in the Green Room: Recovered Dr. Aris Thorne's 1948 medical trial binder from behind the university auditorium stage after his lecture was interrupted.
  • [Session 5] Tampered Ink: Close inspection of the handwritten ledger revealed obvious foul play: someone took a pen and carefully altered the word STABLE to read STALE, turning a successful cure into a fatal poisoning in the history books.
  • [Session 6] Satyr Ambush: Redactor strike teams (hired satyr shock troops) ambushed the party in the auditorium with explicit orders to seize the binder and burn it to ashes.
  • [Session 6] Under Lock & Key: The notebook is secured at the Margin safe house under Professor Dravin's care while he studies how the Redactors alter written records.

Teddy's Warning: The Curse of Carrying Two Relics

Why holding more than one severed thread rings like a dinner bell for every hunter across the worlds.

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 7] Teddy's Rule: Caretaker Teddy warned the party at the cabin: carrying two recovered relics at once marks you. One piece can slip by unnoticed, but two create a pull that hunters can sense from leagues away.
  • [Session 7] Marked Bearers: By carrying both the Raleigh sea-battle stele and Dr. Thorne's 1948 medical notebook, the party is now actively broadcasting their presence to anyone tracking stolen threads.
  • [Session 7] Hunters on the Trail: This scent allows Redactor hounds and specialized trackers (including the six-fingered man) to pinpoint where the party is moving, even through crowded mortal cities.

The Six-Fingered Man: The Redactor Stalker

A shapeshifting hunter with six fingers on each hand who followed the party across a university campus.

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 5] Six Fingers per Hand: Stalked the party across the university grounds; his dead giveaway is having six distinct fingers on each hand.

  • [Session 6] Tricked by a Rabbit: His pursuit was broken when Pierre conjured an illusory glowing rabbit that darted into an alley, baiting the operative away from the party.

Washington D.C. Police Evidence Locker: The Erased Flight Handcuffs

The next severed thread is locked inside a modern police vault: a pair of handcuffs from a 1980s airline flight that vanished from history.

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 4 & 7] Naomi's Recon: Naomi tracked the next piece of the Loom to the evidence room of a modern Metropolitan Police Department precinct in Washington, D.C.
  • [Session 7] Modern Red Tape & Alarms: Unlike dungeons and ancient ruins, this heist means dealing with 1980s municipal security: metal detectors, badge checkpoints, surveillance cameras, booking desks, and armed patrol officers.
  • [Session 8] The Relic: A pair of handcuffs cataloged in connection with an erased 1980s commercial airliner flight that vanished without leaving a trace in air traffic records.
  • [Session 8] Party Boons: Party advances to Level 5; Theodore gave Eusacles an ancestral birthright blessing, while Dravin attuned to a bronze brooch of three pomegranate seeds that shields nearby allies with temporary health.
  • [Session 8] The Redactor Blueprint Patch: Dravin studied the precinct's blueprints and spotted an authorial seam: the records wing is canted thirteen degrees off-axis because Redactors spliced an old evidence locker from an erased timeline directly into the modern building.
  • [Session 8] Sharpie & The Brick Trick: Dravin sharpied his contact details and "PIG" onto Alfie's foot for "legal provenance", then used Meld into Stone to disguise her as a red clay brick. The cops dutifully bagged her as street vandalism evidence and carried her straight past the metal detectors into the processing room.

Physical Anchors: Why Erased Stories Cling to Objects

Why the Redactors can't simply snip a story out of thin air—and why they hunt down and burn the physical relics.

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 2] The Fates' Lore: The Three Fates revealed that an erased story or person cannot completely fade as long as a physical piece of that event still exists in the world.
  • [Session 3 & 5] Anchors in Hand: The Raleigh Greek stele anchors the memory of the sea battle; Dr. Thorne's altered binder anchors the 1948 trial. Without those objects, the memories would dissolve into thin air.
  • [Session 6] Why Redactors Burn Everything: Redactor agents don't just use scissors on the Loom—they hunt down the physical objects and incinerate them, because destroying the object destroys the memory forever.

Thanatos by the Campfire: The Five-Dollar Watch & Pierre's Mother

Death stopped by Eusacles' campfire dream to carve protection runes into a cheap wristwatch and laugh uproariously at Pierre's heritage.

🏛️ Confirmed Canon Facts & In-Game Clues
  • [Session 7] A Visit from Death: Thanatos, the Greek god of death, appeared directly in Eusacles' campfire dream as the party rested in the Lost Roads.
