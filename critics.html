The Critic's Conspiracies & Story Forecasts

Where does the developmental desk believe the GM and players are steering the campaign? These theories operate strictly in the editorial layer, keeping the in-world Canon Thread Tracker 100% factual.

Conspiracy #1 · Provenance · Anchored to TH-01 & TH-02

Alfie Is Carved from the Very Flagship Erased by the Raleigh Stele

When Alfie touched the fractured Aegean stele in the Raleigh museum during Session 4, she experienced an agonizing sensory flashback of burning ports and dark oarsmen—the exact same maritime catastrophe recorded on the stone. The Critic theorizes that Alfie was not crafted from generic driftwood; her timber was salvaged directly from the hull of the ancient Aegean expedition fleet that was excised from American history. If the party successfully stabilizes the stele, the Critic forecasts that Alfie may face an existential choice: maintain her miniature animated identity, or dissolve back into the sea as the timeline corrects itself.

Conspiracy #2 · Macro Conflict · Anchored to TH-03

The Redactors Are Actively Regulating Human Mortality Rates

Why would an unseen hand physically overwrite the word STABLE to STALE in Dr. Thorne's 1948 trial notes? The Critic theorizes that 'The Master' and their Redactor syndicate are not merely hunting ancient mythological gods; they are actively editing modern human scientific history. In the Master's curated timeline, human disease eradication is artificially throttled. A world where Dr. Thorne eradicated the Big Pox in 1948 would have produced an uncurated demographic explosion that derailed the sanitized timeline the Redactors are engineering.

Conspiracy #3 · Character Arc · Anchored to TH-08

Pierre's Mother Is an Elder Gorgon Deity, Not an Italian Mobster

When Eusacles questioned Thanatos regarding Pierre's maternal heritage during the Session 7 campfire dream, the God of Death broke into booming laughter and vanished. Pierre spent the post-interrogation debrief claiming his phrase "Do you know who my mother is?" was merely an Italian mob intimidation tactic. The Critic suspects Pierre is in profound psychological denial: Thanatos laughed because Pierre's mother is an immortal Gorgon matriarch who hid her mortal scion among French stonemasons specifically to keep him off the Redactors' excision rolls.

Conspiracy #4 · Narrative Horizon · Anchored to TH-06

The 1980s Flight Relic Forces the Party into Modern Municipal Surveillance

Up to this point, encounters have taken place in foggy woods, academic halls, and closed museum galleries. By sending the travelers to a high-security Washington, D.C. police evidence vault, the GM is intentionally introducing mortal 20th-century surveillance mechanics: security badges, CCTV cameras, and metropolitan police dispatch. The Critic forecasts that the party's usual reliance on flashy magic (wordcraft, glowing rabbit decoys, divine shields) will trigger immediate mortal police alerts, creating a chaotic three-way escalation between the travelers, municipal law enforcement, and cloaked Redactor trackers.

Editorial Case Study · AI Hallucination Guardrail

The Persephone Anomaly: When Drafting Bots Outrun the Table

During early automated critique passes for Session 5, automated drafting agents took a single brief mention by the GM (Hermes delivering a message from an unnamed traveler descending from Olympus) and hallucinated an entire ungrounded mythos: drafting bot scorecards penalized Professor Dravin for "omitting Persephone interiority" and manufactured a nonexistent "Persephone Signet". The table player (William Webb) was roleplaying a grounded Stanford historian, not an underworld demigod. This serves as the definitive justification for decoupling in-world canon facts (threads.html) from editorial hypotheses (critics.html).
