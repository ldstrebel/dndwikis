# Comic Storyboard: Session 4 (First Day of Classes)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 4: First Day of Classes**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

---

Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch --no 3d, realistic`

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Logistics Class

#### Panel 1: Chalk Snipe
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A comic book panel showing a high-ceilinged, steam-heated classroom. A strict professor stands at a chalkboard. Ignatius, the red-haired fire-elf, is slouching at a wooden desk, looking bored. A piece of white chalk flies across the room, hitting Ignatius square in the forehead. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Sound Effect:* `TAP!`
    *   *Speech Bubble (Professor):* "Focus, Ignatius! Attrition rate in Hangar 12 is calculated, not guessed!"
    *   *Speech Bubble (Ignatius, holding his forehead):* "Ouch! Emotional damage."

#### Panel 2: The Rebuff
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing Lomi approaching Finch Gable, a snobbish student in glasses, in the hallway between classes. Lomi holds out a blank scroll, asking for notes. Finch Gable walks past him with his nose in the air, completely ignoring him. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "Hey, Finch, any chance I could copy your logistics formula?"
    *   *Speech Bubble (Finch):* "I do not share telemetry with boiler-grease, Octoumba."

### Page 2: Trading the Myth

#### Panel 1: Finding Lucky
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel set in a dusty, dimly-lit tavern room. Lucky the grifter sits behind a wooden table, playing with a coin. Ignatius, Lomi, and Iggy stand across the table. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lucky):* "Money's boring. I trade in stories, secrets, and high-potential people. Give me a real secret, and the study guide is yours."

#### Panel 2: The Genesis Myth
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A stylized narrative panel overlaying the characters' faces. Ignatius tells the myth: a massive Sea Dragon (aquatic, turquoise scales) caught a burning Sky Dragon (fiery red wings and embers) in its loving embrace, forming the Ash-Blood Islands in the sea. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Ignatius):* "My islands were formed when the Sea Dragon caught the Sky Dragon... keeping the heat alive."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lucky, grinning):* "A genuine myth. I like it. Deal."

### Page 3: The Receipt Cliffhanger

#### Panel 1: The Backroom
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A suspenseful comic panel. Lucky slides the study guide scroll across the table, then looks at Iggy's stolen/recovered exam receipt. Lucky's smirk disappears, his eyes wide in sudden seriousness. He points to a dark door leading to the backroom. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lucky):* "Wait. Where did you get this receipt? This layout... this isn't a standard exam registry. We need to talk in the back. Now."
    *   *Dungeon Master Cameo (Leaning in from peeled bottom-left corner):* "The plot thickens. What did Iggy's test receipt actually register him for?"
