# Comic Storyboard: Session 3 (The Lounge & The Ambush)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 3: The Celestial Lounge and The Ambush**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

---

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Crown and Ruin (Celestial Lounge)

#### Panel 1: The Dice Game
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A vibrant, high-energy panel set in the opulent Celestial Lounge. Lomi the Octoumba, grinning confidently, rolls glowing dice across a green velvet table during a game of 'Crown and Ruin'. Valentine Sterling, looking nervous, and Ember, looking excited, watch. Food platters and grease shots are scattered on the table. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "Looks like Ruin for you, Val! Pay up."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Val, blushing):* "Ah, clean win, Lomi. You're direct."

#### Panel 2: Val's Intel
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing Val leaning in to whisper to Lomi as they clean up the game. Val looks anxious, glancing over his shoulder. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Val):* "The upcoming exam... I wrote it myself. It's tough. I sold an annotated study guide to Lucky the grifter. You should find him."

### Page 2: The Alley Ambush

#### Panel 1: Cornered in the Dark
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A dark, high-contrast comic panel set in a narrow brick alleyway. Britt the fungal-turtle is cornered by three cloaked figures (Azer, Nickel, and Tus). Azer holds a glowing electrical stun-pike, while Tus reaches for Britt's pocket watch. Britt shields her chest, eyes wide. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "Leaving the lounge, Britt is cornered in the dark alleys of the Upper Core..."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Azer):* "Hand over the watch and the receipts, turtle-kin."

#### Panel 2: The Struggle
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A close-up panel showing Britt kicking Azer in the shin, wrenching her arm free to save her heirloom pocket watch, but Tus snatches her exam receipt papers. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Sound Effect:* `KICK!`
    *   *Speech Bubble (Britt):* "You are NOT taking this watch! It's my family's!"

### Page 3: Lomi and Iggy's Rescue

#### Panel 1: Breaking the Ribs
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A dynamic action panel. Lomi the Octoumba slams his heavy shoulder directly into Azer's side, cracking his ribs with a concussive shockwave. Iggy, scaling a pipe above, throws a metallic cog that sparks off Tus's head. The ambushers stumble back in pain. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Sound Effect (Vibrant yellow):* `CRUNCH!`
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi, roaring):* "Get off our friend!"
    *   *Speech Bubble (Azer, coughing):* "Retreat! Let's go!"

#### Panel 2: Safe but Scarred
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A warm panel showing the group under a gaslamp. Lomi checks on Britt, who is catching her breath, clutching her watch. Iggy sits on a crate nearby. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Britt):* "They took my exam receipt. But... I kept the watch."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "We find Lucky tomorrow. He's got the study guide, and he's going to tell us who sent those thugs."
    *   *Dungeon Master Cameo (Leaning in from peeled bottom-right corner):* "Lomi's punch broke a rib. Word gets around: don't mess with the boiler crew."
