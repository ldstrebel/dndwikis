# Comic Storyboard: Session 5 (Cards on the Table)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 5: Cards on the Table**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

---

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: High-Stakes Deals

#### Panel 1: Lucky's Anomalies
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A comic book panel set in Lucky's cluttered, backroom warehouse closet. Lucky the grifter sits across from Iggy the trench-kin and Ignatius the red-haired fire-elf. Lucky gestures to a diagram on the wall. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lucky):* "I track anomalies. The purple lightning, the weird tech... and you, Iggy. You don't use standard Harmony batteries. Show me what you've got."

#### Panel 2: The Trade
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A close-up panel showing Iggy placing a tiny, intricate clockwork beetle gadget on the table. The beetle's brass gears click and turn. Ignatius stands with crossed arms like a professional manager, nodding. Lucky stares at it, completely charmed. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Iggy):* "Clockwork. Runs on springs. No crystals."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Ignatius):* "That's high-tier craftsmanship. We want the annotated study guide and the Reso Race intel."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lucky, grinning):* "Deal. This is beautiful."

### Page 2: Lomi's Grease-Grog Deal

#### Panel 1: Ambrosia of Luck
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing Lomi holding up a bottle of blue-glowing grease-grog ('Ambrosia of Luck') in front of Lucky's face. Lucky sniffs it, coughing at the fumes, but looks impressed. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "Ambrosia of Luck. Furnace-grade grog. We sell it in the Bleachers on race day, and we split the profits."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lucky):* "Phew! Smells like a boiler explosion. I love it. I'll set up the distribution."

### Page 3: Scouting the Apex Ring

#### Panel 1: The Spire Mechanics
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A wide comic panel showing the empty stone bleachers of the Apex Ring stadium under bright daylight. Lomi, Britt, and Aggie look over the arena floor, which is split between dry mud and turquoise water. Large metal boxes (spire launchers) sit flush with the floor. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "Scouting the Apex Ring, the group discovers the race mechanics..."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "The spires launch randomly from mobile boxes under the turf. But Juxta's Spire—the lift-stone—tends to pop near the vegetation."

#### Panel 2: Prism Water Security
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing Lomi pointing to a gate guarded by guards where a canal of glowing, iridescent liquid (Prism Water) flows. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "Watch the maintenance gates. That's Prism Water. It reads true roles and identities. We can't sneak through there."
    *   *Dungeon Master Cameo (Leaning in from peeled bottom-left corner):* "Ignatius and Iggy have a different plan for sneak-in: a turban, a trench coat, and a lot of confidence."
