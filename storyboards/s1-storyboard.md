# Comic Storyboard: Session 1 (First Day)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 1: First Day**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

---

## 👤 Character Reference Prompts

### 1. Seraphina "Serra" Vox (Noble Spying as Commoner)
> **Prompt:** `Character design sheet for Seraphina "Serra" Vox, a wealthy human noble girl pretending to be a commoner. She has long, wavy dark hair, intelligent violet eyes, and wears a simple dark blue student tunic that she tries to make look plain, though it fits perfectly. Turnaround sheet, expressive face. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch --no 3d, realistic`

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Scattered Housing

#### Panel 1: Dorm Assignments
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A comic book panel showing a bustling courtyard outside the Vumbua administration office. Five newly enrolled first-year students—Britt the fungal-turtle, Ignatius the red-haired fire-elf, Lomi the Octoumba, and Iggy the tiny trench-kin—stare at a large wooden notice board listing dorm assignments. Crowds of students bustle in the background. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "Housing assignments scatter the newly formed group across the modular districts of Vumbua..."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Iggy):* "Block 12. Far away."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "I'm in Block 99. The boiler rooms are right below. Smells like grease and home."

#### Panel 2: Saying Goodbye
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing the friends splitting up. Ignatius is slinging a canvas duffel bag over his shoulder, nodding to the turtle-kin Britt and Aggie. Iggy is already walking away, dragging his oversized trench coat. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Ignatius):* "See y'all tomorrow. Don't get lost in the pipes."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Britt):* "We'll meet at the central courtyard at noon."

### Page 2: Iggy's Sky Climb

#### Panel 1: The Crane
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A tall vertical panel. Iggy, the small trench-kin with glowing cyan goggles, climbs hand-over-hand up the metal ladder of a massive, rusted copper loading crane towering over Hangar Block 12. Below him, the lights of Vumbua flicker as twilight falls. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "Seeking space and a view, Iggy scales the highest scaffold he can find..."
    *   *Sound Effect:* `CLANK CLANK CLANK`

#### Panel 2: Touching the Sky
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A wide panel from behind Iggy sitting on the edge of the crane's boom, dangling his legs. His goggles reflect the vast twilight sky of the frontier, stars beginning to shine. The sprawling modular city of Vumbua is laid out beneath him. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Iggy, quiet):* "To touch the sky. Comfortable."
    *   *Dungeon Master Cameo (Leaning in from peeled top-right corner):* "From up here, the city looks like a puzzle box waiting to be opened."

### Page 3: Meeting Serra Vox

#### Panel 1: The Courtyard Encounter
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A bright morning panel in the central courtyard. A human girl with long dark hair, Seraphina "Serra" Vox, approaches the group with a warm smile, holding a platter of pastries. The group looks at her suspiciously. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Serra):* "Hi! I saw you guys at the trials. I'm Serra. I figured we could all use an ally on the first day."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi, squinting):* "Free food? What's the catch?"

#### Panel 2: The Slip-Up
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A close-up panel focusing on Serra's nervous expression. She is laughing off a question, but her hand-gestures betray a high-society posture. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Serra):* "Oh, no catch! Just a common first-year trying to survive... like everyone else."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Britt):* "You don't talk like the outskirts, Serra."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Serra, blushing):* "I, uh, studied a lot of pamphlets!"
