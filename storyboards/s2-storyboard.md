# Comic Storyboard: Session 2 (The Bonfire)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 2: The Bonfire**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

---

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Stacking logs at Block 99

#### Panel 1: Geometric Log Stacking
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A warm-toned comic book panel showing Cassius Thorne, an earnest student, carefully and obsessively stacking logs into a perfect hexagonal bonfire grid in the Block 99 courtyard at dusk. Lomi sits nearby on an empty crate, holding a flask and grinning. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "A bonfire at Block 99 becomes the stage for new friendships and philosophical debates..."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lomi):* "You've got a real eye for structural load, Cassius. That's a beautiful stack."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Cassius, blushing):* "If the geometry is off, the resonance decays unevenly. It's just simple thermodynamics!"

### Page 2: The Speculators' Debate

#### Panel 1: Elements and Doctrines
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A wide comic book panel of the bonfire roaring with orange fire. Around the fire, Percy (noble blond) gestures grandly while Iron-Jaw Jax (a rugged dwarf) crosses his arms skeptically. Bramble (Mizizi druid) watches quietly, poking a stick in the fire. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Percy):* "The Sixfold Theory proves the missing Earth, Air, and Water nodes must exist! Harmony cannot remain stable without them."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Jax):* "Theoretical junk. The Loom doesn't care about balance, it cares about amps."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Serra Vox, whispering to Lomi):* "Under the Inverse Power Doctrine, Lady Ignis holds the highest voting power because she represents the newest integrated node."

### Page 3: Zephyr's Lightning

#### Panel 1: Lightning Strike
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A dramatic, high-impact comic book panel. A sudden, thick bolt of purple lightning cracks down from a completely clear twilight sky, striking Zephyr (a young student) dead-on. The impact lights up the courtyard in brilliant violet light. The surrounding students scream and shield their eyes. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Sound Effect (Vibrant purple):* `CRACK-BOOM!`
    *   *Speech Bubble (Ignatius):* "Zephyr!"

#### Panel 2: Unharmed
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing Zephyr standing in the smoking grass, completely unharmed, her clothes slightly singed but her eyes wide and glowing with lingering purple energy. In the foreground, Rill, a Wadi researcher with green-tinted hair, rushes out of the crowd to pull her away. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Rill):* "She's okay. Everyone stand back! Zephyr, come with me."
    *   *Dungeon Master Cameo (Leaning in from peeled bottom-left corner):* "Zephyr can trigger purple lightning when stressed, but she walks away unscathed every time."
