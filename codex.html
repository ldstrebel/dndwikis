Session 1: The Bus from Vegas & The Library of the Fates

Read Story Chapter →

Quick Session Summary

Displaced from a midnight Las Vegas Greyhound bus into dimensional freefall, four disparate strangers awaken inside the marble colonnades of the celestial Library of the Fates. As predatory ink-beasts known as Correctors tear through the stacks to consume severed timeline threads, the Three Fates rapidly weave Alfie from driftwood and sea-thread to bolster the frontline. Escaping through a cosmic tapestry rift, the survivors tumble into the foggy Appalachian threshold of The Margin.

Dramatis Personae (In This Session)

Alfie · Pierre · Prof. Edward Dravin · Eusacles · The Three Fates (Clotho, Lachesis, Atropos) · Correctors (Ink-Beasts)

Locale (In This Session)

Las Vegas Highway Basin (Mortal Plane) $\rightarrow$ The Celestial Colonnade & Library of the Fates $\rightarrow$ The Appalachian Fog Threshold (The Margin).

Scene Summary

  • Act I (Chapters 1–2): The Nevada night journey fractures; the Greyhound bus falls into planar freefall, depositing passengers onto celestial marble.
  • Act II (Chapters 3–5): Correctors swarm the stacks; Pierre, Dravin, and Eusacles mount an impromptu defensive line against ink-draining hounds.
  • Act III (Chapters 6–9): The Fates spin Alfie from weathered timber, sailcloth, and drowned sailor threads; Alfie enters combat with her needle rapier.
  • Act IV (Chapters 10–12): The colonnade buckles under reality collapse; the party leaps through a tapestry seam, landing in the fog of the Appalachian haven.

Session 7: The Interrogation, The Redactor & The Dream of Thanatos

Quick Session Summary

In a university arcade, the party interrogates their captive Redactor, learning that 'The Master' curates a utopian timeline and targets them as living anomaly anchors.
