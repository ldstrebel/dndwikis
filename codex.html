Session 1: The Bus from Vegas & The Library of the Fates

Read Story Chapter →

Quick Session Summary

Displaced from a midnight Las Vegas Greyhound bus into dimensional freefall, four disparate strangers awaken inside the marble colonnades of the celestial Library of the Fates. As predatory ink-beasts known as Correctors tear through the stacks to consume severed timeline threads, the Three Fates rapidly weave Alfie from driftwood and sea-thread to bolster the frontline. Escaping through a cosmic tapestry rift, the survivors tumble into the foggy Appalachian threshold of The Margin.

Dramatis Personae (In This Session)

Locale (In This Session)

Las Vegas Highway Basin (Mortal Plane) → The Celestial Colonnade & Library of the Fates → The Appalachian Fog Threshold (The Margin).

Scene Breakdown & Dramatic Arc (Swain MRU)

  • Act I (Chapters 1–2) · The Inciting Fracture: The Nevada night journey shears apart; radio reports of a D.C.-bound flight are cut short as the Greyhound plunges into celestial void, depositing four travelers onto the glowing marble of the Fates' library.
  • Act II (Chapters 3–5) · The Corrector Swarm: Predatory ink-beasts breach the stacks to devour severed threads; Pierre, Dravin, and Eusacles mount an impromptu perimeter defense using chill touch, silver chimes, and brawler fists.
  • Act III (Chapters 6–9) · The Driftwood Awakening: With the Loom collapsing, the Three Fates rapidly weave Alfie from driftwood and sea-cloth; Alfie awakens with 458 years of grain memory and fences through the beast frontline.
  • Act IV (Chapters 10–12) · The Tapestry Leap: Reality buckles; the party leaps through a severed cosmic tapestry rift, tumbling into the foggy Appalachian sanctuary threshold of The Margin.

Quick Session Summary

Ambushed inside the museum gallery by serpent-crowned Gorgon Redactors, the company mounts a chaotic defense using improvised baguette flash-bombs and high-altitude clay urn drops. Session 5: The Forgotten Trail & The Mad Doctor's Lecture

Read Story Chapter →

Quick Session Summary

A dispatch delivered via Hermes Express brings intelligence of anomalous activity tied to Dr. Aris Thorne. Traveling across the Lost Roads to University University in Pennsylvania, the company infiltrates a turbulent lecture hall amidst student protests over the controversial medical historian. Infiltrating the green room, Dravin recovers Thorne's altered 1948 trial notes—where 'STABLE' was retroactively overwritten to 'STALE' by an unseen hand—just as satyr strike teams smash through the doors.

Scene Breakdown & Dramatic Arc (Swain MRU)
