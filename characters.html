The Displaced Travelers (Player Characters)

Alfie

PC

The Driftwood Duelist · Wordcraft Mage

Player: Sophie Foreman Noone

An animated driftwood construct less than a foot tall, carved from weathered maritime timber with sailcloth joints, polished seashell buttons for eyes, and a sharp steel fishing hook for a hand. Alfie speaks in a confident Cockney sailor cadence and suffers from visceral flashbacks of freezing brine, dark oarsmen, and burning coastal ports whenever she touches ancient nautical stone tablets.

⚡ Core Abilities & Gear

  • Wordcraft: Reality-tampering Fate power altering spells or concepts by one letter.
  • Needle Rapier: Razor-sharp sewing needle wielded with pinpoint fencing precision.
  • Tendon Sever: Hook-hand strikes crippling large monsters at the Achilles heel.
  • Gear: Sewing-needle rapier, steel hook hand, sailcloth vest, seashell button eyes.

🎙️ Voice & Cadence

Raspy, irreverent Cockney accent with high rhythmic energy. Quick-witted, fiercely loyal to Pierre, and prone to mocking bloated authority figures while perched atop someone's shoulder.

Related Quest: TH-01

Prof. Edward Dravin

PC

The Classical Historian · Stanford Epigrapher

Player: William Webb

Professor of Classical Greek history at Stanford University. Pulled unexpectedly into the Margin from the Las Vegas bus, Dravin acts as the academic and forensic backbone of the travelers, deciphering ancient Aegean dialects, cataloging recovered historical artifacts, and casting abjuration and necromantic wards under pressure.

⚡ Core Abilities & Gear

  • 3-Seed Pomegranate Signet Pin: Unlocked at Level 5; projects an aura granting 10 temporary hit points to all allies within 30 ft.
  • Level 5 Infiltration Spellcraft: Meld into Stone (used to turn Alfie into an evidence brick) alongside abjuration wards.
  • Epigraphic Deciphering & Blueprints: Rapid analysis of ancient Aegean scripts and structural municipal blueprints.
  • Gear: Pomegranate signet pin, leather field satchel, classical lexicons, Sharpie (for legal provenance).

🎙️ Voice & Cadence

Measured, articulate professorial tone. Balances intellectual curiosity with pragmatic panic when forced into frontline combat. The party's scholarly anchor.

Related Quests: TH-02, TH-03

Eusacles

PC

The Aegis Veteran · Thanatos Watch Bearer

Player: John Hagey

A rugged Las Vegas brawler and streetwise gambler whose cheap five-dollar quartz wristwatch was physically carved with glowing divine protection runes during a campfire visitation by Thanatos. Eusacles acts as the frontline shield and pragmatic enforcer of the group.

⚡ Core Abilities & Gear

  • Thanatos Warded Watch: Emits kinetic force fields, transforms into a morningstar, and grants +2 AC when bloodied.
  • Level 5 Paladin Progression: Rectified starting equipment (full armor & shield toggled on); heavy frontline defense.
  • Theodore's Birthright Boon: Ancient sanctuary blessing bestowed at The Margin prior to the Washington D.C. deployment.
  • Gear: Rune-carved quartz watch, heavy paladin plate, shield, taped hand wraps.

🎙️ Voice & Cadence

Gruff, world-weary American cadence with dry humor. Direct and protective, preferring blunt action over academic contemplation.

Related Quest: TH-08

Pierre

PC

The Parisian Stonemason · Gorgon Bloodline

Player: Luke S

A cynical Parisian stonemason who boarded a Greyhound bus in Las Vegas only to plunge into planar freefall. Despite clinging stubbornly to physical realism, Pierre manifests serpentine shadow auras and petrifying gaze magic under stress, pointing toward an ancient Gorgon maternal lineage that Thanatos found intensely amusing.

⚡ Core Abilities & Gear

  • Gorgon Glare: Brief flashes of petrification slowing or solidifying foes.
  • Stonecraft Mastery: Intuitive structural knowledge of stone vaults and masonry.
  • Patronus Distraction: Conjurative rabbit decoy capable of fooling hostile trackers.
  • Gear: Heavy masonry hammer, chisels, thick denim work jacket.

🎙️ Voice & Cadence

Sardonic French accent, deeply skeptical of divine claims. Constantly acts as the exasperated straight man to Alfie's chaotic antics.

Related Quests: TH-05, TH-08

Sanctuary Allies & Key NPCs

Dr. Aris Thorne

1948 Trial Historian & Lecturer
NPC

An eccentric medical historian whose lecture on the 1948 Big Pox clinical trials at University University was interrupted by a Redactor satyr strike team. His recovered notes binder is at the center of Campaign Thread TH-03.

Key Appearance: Session 5 & 6 · Auditorium Incursion

Naomi

Margin Operations Coordinator
NPC

The tactical coordinator of the Margin Wardens. Naomi maps anomalous timeline seams, organizes party field infiltrations, and runs the Anomaly Board tracking Redactor incursions.

Key Appearance: Active Across Sessions 2, 4, 7

Mike

Quartermaster & Logistics Lead
NPC

Sanctuary quartermaster who oversees survival supplies, field weaponry, and transit vehicles navigating the volatile edges of the Lost Roads.

Key Appearance: Sessions 2 & 4 Debriefs

Rosa

Library Archival Specialist
NPC

Assists in cataloging recovered timeline fragments and damaged epigraphy within the Library of the Fates, working closely with Prof. Dravin on deciphering ancient Aegean text.

Key Appearance: Sessions 2 & 7

Susanna & Teddy

Margin Sanctuary Civilians
NPC

Residents displaced from excised timeline points who found refuge in the Margin. Teddy maintains the campfire perimeter where Thanatos manifested in Session 7.

Key Appearance: Sessions 2, 6, 7

Celestial Entities & Cosmic Patrons

The Three Fates

Weavers of the Epigraphy

Clotho, Lachesis, and Atropos. Ancient weavers dwelling in the sanctuary's core library who originally spun Alfie from driftwood and threads of drowned sailors.

Session 1 & 2 Origins

Hermes

Courier of the Lost Roads

The divine messenger whose express courier delivery cuts across planar boundaries. Demands transit payments for cross-dimensional parchment dispatches delivered to the Margin.

Session 5 Interventions

Thanatos

Lord of the Seam & Finality

The personification of peaceful passage and cosmic conclusion. Personally engraved Eusacles' watch and laughed knowingly when asked about Pierre's maternal lineage.

Session 7 Campfire Manifestation

🐙 Majora

The Relic Stalker · Cosmic Adversary
Origin: Session 8

A terrifying, faceless, tentacled cosmic entity that relentlessly stalks individuals who collect shattered historical timeline relics. Introduced in Session 8 alongside Blades-in-the-Dark tracking clocks, Majora's manifestation is accelerated whenever timeline seekers come into physical contact with planar residue or linger near unaligned relics.

Campaign Factions

The Margin Wardens

Allied Sanctuary Defenders

An organized cadre of displaced survivors, scouts, and scholars holding the border perimeter of the timeless refuge known as the Margin against Redactor sweeps.

The Redactors

Primary Adversary Force

A ruthless metaphysical syndicate systematically locating historical anomalies and burning their physical anchors to irrevocably redact timeline branches from reality.
